    Selection boundaries¶

    • Password-entry screens and output containing HTTP cookies are not published. The relevant configuration and HTTPS result are described in text instead.
    • Screenshots from the retired Docker/kind/SQL lab are not evidence of this rebuilt platform. Personal storage cleanup screenshots are also outside scope.
    • Later registration, successful SSH login and enabled repositories were reported in text; screenshots of those results were not supplied.
    • RHEL metadata refresh and update-check success were reported in text; no screenshot is claimed for those results.

    The images include local lab names, private network addresses and virtual device identifiers. They contain no employer infrastructure screenshots.
